How It Works

Three steps. No long story.

From posting your bill to getting it funded — it's straightforward.

1

Upload your bill

Snap a photo or upload a PDF of your school fee, medical invoice, or work tool receipt.

2

AI verifies it

Our AI audits your receipt for authenticity, validates institution data, and redacts sensitive info.

3

Sponsor pays directly

Sponsors fund your bill straight to your bank account or institution. Zero middleman.

AI-Powered

Pure receipts. No long story.

Every school fee, medical bill, and work invoice is audited by AI before it goes live. Duplicate detection, institution validation, and sensitive data redaction — built in.

  • OCR receipt scanning and validation
  • Duplicate image detection (pHash)
  • BVN/NIN auto-redaction for privacy
Non-Custodial

Zero middleman. Direct to account.

Sponsors fund requests straight into the recipient's verified bank account or institutional payment channel. No platform custody, no delays, no trust issues.

  • Direct P2P bank transfers
  • OPay, Moniepoint, GTBank, Palmpay supported
  • Transparent — sponsors see exactly where money goes
Padi Need

Urgent 2k? Quick micro-funding.

Not everything needs an invoice. Padi Need is a lightweight, text-first feed for urgent daily needs — data, transport fare, airtime. Post it, tag it, get it settled.

  • No invoice upload required
  • Quick tags: Urgent 2k, Data & Airtime, Transport
  • AI-polished descriptions for better reach
