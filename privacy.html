Legal & Security

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026 • Platform: Bill Me (Android & PWA)

1. Introduction

Welcome to Bill Me ("we," "our," or "us"). Bill Me is a direct bill-sponsorship and community support platform that connects individuals seeking assistance with verified unpaid invoices or urgent needs ("Beneficiaries") with sponsors willing to fund those bills directly ("Sponsors" or "Donors").

Because Bill Me operates on a zero-middleman, non-gateway model, we do not hold, escrow, or process fiat funds. Instead, we provide automated document verification, anti-fraud fingerprinting, receipt auditing, and moderated communication tools.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we process it, and how we protect your privacy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account & Authentication Information

  • Google Sign-In Data: When you sign in using Google Authentication (via Android Credential Manager or Web OAuth), we collect your unique user identifier (uid), display name, email address, and profile photo URL.
  • Profile Preferences: Optional profile details you provide, such as your display name, biography, phone number, avatar image, and notification preferences.

2.2 Bill, Invoice & Payout Information (Beneficiaries)

  • Uploaded Invoice Documents: Photos or PDF files of unpaid bills (e.g., school tuition schedules, medical invoices, exam fee notices, work equipment quotes).
  • Extracted Bill Metadata: Beneficiary name, issuing institution/vendor name, invoice/matriculation reference number, itemized fee breakdown, amount due, currency, and due date.
  • Receiving Bank Account Details: Institution bursary bank details or personal receiving bank name, 10-digit NUBAN account number, and verified account name so Sponsors can send transfers directly.

2.3 Sponsorship & Transfer Receipt Information (Sponsors)

  • Transfer Receipts: Screenshots or PDFs of bank/fintech debit transfer slips uploaded to confirm a sponsorship.
  • Receipt Audit Metadata: Extracted NIBSS Session ID or bank transaction reference, transfer amount, recipient account number, and sponsorship display preferences (including whether you choose to sponsor Anonymously).

2.4 Anti-Duplicate & Fraud-Prevention Fingerprints

  • Perceptual Image Hashes (imagePhash): A 64-bit mathematical hash computed on-device from your uploaded document to detect duplicate visual uploads.
  • Composite Metadata Fingerprints (metadataFingerprint): A cryptographic SHA-256 hash derived from normalized institution name, invoice reference, and amount due to prevent duplicate publishing across accounts.
  • Fraud Audit Logs: If an account attempts to upload or publish a bill already registered to another user, we log a restricted security record (fraud_flags) containing the original bill ID, attempting UID, and match stage.

2.5 In-App Safe Chat Data

  • Chat Messages & Metadata: Messages exchanged between Sponsors and Beneficiaries, participant UIDs, anonymity flags, timestamps, and automated safety risk classifications (SAFE, SUSPICIOUS, BLOCKED).

2.6 Device & Connectivity Information

  • Network Status: Real-time online/offline connectivity state used to enable cached viewing and prevent data loss during form submission.

3. Strictly Prohibited Sensitive Identifiers (Automatic Redaction)

Bill Me never requests and strictly prohibits the submission of sensitive government or banking credentials, including:

  • Bank Verification Numbers (BVN)
  • National Identification Numbers (NIN)
  • Social Security Numbers (SSN)
  • Bank PINs, OTPs, CVVs, or online banking passwords

Our client-side and AI pipelines automatically scan for and redact 11-digit national/banking ID patterns and block chat messages that attempt to share or solicit these identifiers.

4. How We Use Your Information & Automated AI Processing

  • Public Bill Listings & Direct P2P Transfers: Bills published on Bill Me (and associated payment account details) are visible to authenticated users so Sponsors can inspect the invoice and transfer funds directly to the institution or Beneficiary.
  • Automated AI Document Auditing: Uploaded invoices and transfer receipts are processed via Google Gemini AI (Firebase AI Logic) to:
    • Verify whether an uploaded document is an unpaid invoice vs. an already-paid receipt (already-paid receipts are blocked from being posted as bills).
    • Extract line items, totals, and institutional letterheads.
    • Audit uploaded sponsor transfer slips for matching NUBAN account numbers, amounts, and unique Session IDs (settled_receipts).
  • Duplicate & Replay Prevention: We use imagePhash, bill_fingerprints, and settled_receipts locks to ensure the same invoice or transfer receipt cannot be reused across multiple listings or accounts.
  • AI Chat Moderation & Safety Shield: Chat messages are evaluated in real time to block harassment, predatory demands, off-platform coercion, or sharing of sensitive personal identifiers.
  • Public & Link-Only Campaign Pools (/giveaway/:slug): When you create or join a Campaign Pool, we associate your bill or sponsorship with that pool's slug and update aggregate pool counters (total bills sorted and total amount disbursed).
  • Public Proof Cards ("PAID O!!"): When a bill is sponsored, we mask the Beneficiary's last name (e.g., "Ejeh P.") on downloadable/shareable proof cards to protect Beneficiary dignity while verifying impact.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

  • With Other Platform Users: Published bills, invoice snapshots, and receiving bank account numbers are visible to authenticated users for sponsorship purposes. If a Sponsor enables Anonymous Sponsorship, their display name is masked as "Anonymous Sponsor" on public listings and chats.
  • Service Providers: We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase AI Logic) and Vercel (Web hosting and Android App Links routing) to operate the platform.
  • No Sale of Personal Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.
  • Legal & Fraud Compliance: We may disclose information if required by law or to investigate verified financial fraud, forgery, or threats to user safety.

6. Data Storage, Security & Retention

  • Zero-Trust Security Rules: All database reads and writes are enforced by strict Cloud Firestore security rules requiring authentication, schema validation, owner-only bill deletion, and participant-only chat access.
  • Client-Side Compression: Invoice images and transfer receipts are compressed on-device prior to upload to minimize data exposure and bandwidth usage.
  • Retention & Deletion: You may delete your open bill listings at any time from your Profile screen, which also releases the associated bill_fingerprints lock. You may request full account deletion by contacting our support team.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA), GDPR, and CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Access and review the personal data associated with your account.
  • Update your profile and payout details or toggle your anonymity in sponsorships and chats.
  • Delete your published bills and request erasure of your account data.

8. Permissions Used (Android & Web)

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Optional; used only when you explicitly choose to capture a photo of an invoice or transfer receipt.
  • System Photo & Document Picker: Uses Android's zero-permission Photo Picker and Storage Access Framework (image/*, application/pdf)—Bill Me never requests broad background access to your photo library.
  • Internet & Network State: Used to sync real-time Firestore updates and detect offline mode.
